Luna Electric was started in the Spring of 2018 by Josue Luna. After completing an Electrical Apprenticeship, Josue rose through the ranks from Leadman to General Foreman and eventually to Project Manager.

Having worked for some of the best electrical contractors in San Diego, he was involved in research laboratories in Sorrento Valley and Indian casino hotels in the East County. Seeing the need for electrical contractors trained in the most current building technologies, Josue established Luna Electric.

  • Residential From panel upgrades to new EV home charging stations, Luna Electric offers a wide range of residential electrical services that will keep your home electrical system performing at peak performance.
  • Commercial Offering commercial electrical services in a wide range of areas. From Title 24 compliance to energy management. New construction, tenant improvements, code compliance — Luna Electric has the expertise and experience to implement and manage these challenging opportunities.
  • Service From time to time, things don't work like they should. Lights don't come on, motors make strange noises and sometimes you may see sparks. Let our trained professionals diagnose and fix these issues in an expert manner.
